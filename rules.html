Rules to Know Before You Hunt

Everything you need to know before you hunt, trade, or claim a dino.

General Rules

Triceratops’ #1 priority is safe Discord behavior and community conduct. Please read over the following for general Behavior & Conduct rules.

Always

Respectful behavior is expected at all times, both in-person and within our online community.

Be respectful to staff members during drops.

Keep DMs to the Instagram strictly related to Davis Triceratops, do not send personal DMs to our official club Instagram.

Never

Do not post NSFW content in the server. This is an immediately bannable offense.

No rude, slanderous, offensive, or hateful comments, indecent or inappropriate content, or slurs of any kind.

Do not reveal staff location during drops or their individual time.

Do not DM staff unless given explicit permission by staff.

Hunt Rules

Any confusion on how a scavenger works and operates? Read the following to prepare yourself with these hunt rules so your hunt can go smoothly.

Outdoors Only

Most dinos or event materials will be hidden outdoors.

  • Dinos will only be hidden inside if stated specifically by the dropper.
  • Be respectful to those around you. The goal is safety and giving others space.
  • If dinos are hidden inside, be mindful of your surroundings and other people.

Random Timing

Daily dino drops occur at random.

  • Timing depends on the Dropper’s personal discretion, schedule, and time permitted.
  • Drops will occur only during daylight hours.

Adhere to Dino Limits

Each person is limited to:

1 Monthly Dino per month 1 Milestone Dino 1 Aggieceratops Dino 1 of each type of Special Dino
  • These limits promote fairness and equal opportunity for all event participants.
  • Anyone caught trying to bypass the dino limits will be banned from the server.

Accessible Spots Only

Never endanger yourself to access event materials.

  • Do not trample or damage plants or property of any sort.
  • Dinos will not be placed deeply into bushes.
  • If a dino is displaced somewhere hard to reach, do not risk your personal safety to retrieve it.
  • Stay on the path, stay out of construction zones, and leave campus spaces as you found them.

Don’t Ask About Drops

Do not ask when or where drops may occur.

  • Pleading and excessively requesting for drops may result in corrective action.

Remember that each of our hardworking staff commits their personal time and effort to allow for drops to occur.

Flash Sale Rules

For EACH month’s flash sale, you can buy one regular and one medium dino.

  • This rule resets every year.

e.g. one regular and one medium dino in every flash sale of 2026 – that resets at the end of 2026 and starts over for 2027.

Flash Sale Rules

Flash sales are one of your only opportunities to directly purchase a dino from the club. To know more about how to obtain one, read the information below.

Listing hours
12am – 10am 5pm – 11:59pm

Flash sales no longer run for the whole 24 hours on the last day of each month. Instead, listing giveaways will run from 12am–10pm & 5pm–11;59pm.

1

Do NOT enter a raffle if you have already won a dino (of the same size) within the flash sale.

2

Do participate and interact with staff via emoji reactions in the #listings channel during flash sales! This will make the whole process fun for both staff and general members!

3

Behave respectfully and properly during flash sales! Do NOT demand and force our staff to list sales.

Claimed a dino?
Here are your next steps:

The next actionable steps post-claim!

  1. After finding a monthly triceratops, make a post in the #found-triceratops text channel. This post should include a photo proof of the triceratops.

  2. Remember to grab your monthly triceratops parents role in the #assign-roles based on the triceratops you just claimed!

  3. Enjoy your new dino! We crafted each one with love and hope you like your one of a kind Triceratops dino.

Common Rules Questions

What if I miss a monthly dino drop? Will I have more chances to hunt for more?

If you miss a monthly dino drop, do not worry; there will certainly be more to come! Dino drops of a certain month will last until the second to last day of the month, so be on the lookout for more drops from our staff.

Staff often drop different types of dinos within a hunt. What if I find a dino type that I already have or am not searching for?

If you find a dino you do not want, carefully hide the dino in the same place. If you are unable to, you may also return the dino to the staff who is in charge of the current hunt.

Any questions on these rules, or about anything at all?

Open a ticket in the #modmail channel, and use the command /tickets open to ask our staff any questions!

Still have questions or need more clarification? Ask in #rules-help on Discord.
