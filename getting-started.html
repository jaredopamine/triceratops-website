When & Where Drops Happen

Drops can happen anytime, anywhere. Here's what to expect!

No Fixed Schedule

Drops happen spontaneously throughout the week, most often on weekdays at UC Davis.

Staff post in #new-drop-notifs whenever they're free.

Beyond The Campus

Dino drops can occur outside of campus and during weekends.

They will most likely happen in local events around Davis, such as the Farmers Market on Saturday and annual festivals.

Daylight Only

Worried about heavy time commitments? Being a dino-thusiast is flexible!

Many of our events and drops are flexible and we don’t require attendance at meetings! You can be involved at your own time and convenience!

What Happens During Drops

During our general drops that are posted in #🔔new-drop-notifs🔔, our staff will disclose what type of drop is happening.

Want more details? Check the "Drop Types" section under [...] for a quick reference list.

Still confused? Ask the staff member running the event, or head to #general-qna under "Support and Inquiries" on Discord for help from our mods.

Please don't DM staff directly, our channels are the fastest way to get answers!

What To Do

  • Be respectful — most dinos are hidden outdoors to give everyone space
  • Indoor hides only happen if the dropper says so — stay aware of others
  • Trust the placement — every dino is reachable, never risk yourself

What Not To Do

  • Don't damage plants, bushes, or property while searching
  • Can't reach it safely? Leave it
  • Don't ask staff when or where the next drop is
  • Don't pressure staff — they're volunteering their time

Dino Limits

These regulations are in place to promote fairness and equal opportunity for all event participants.
Anyone caught trying to bypass the dino limits will be banned from the server.

  • Monthly Dino

    1 Monthly Dino / Person (Each Month)

    In a new year, this rule doesn't reset. Event Dinos DO NOT count as monthly dinos.

  • Milestone Dino

    1 Milestone Dino / Person

    Each member can be awarded 1 Milestone Dino, ever.

  • Aggieceratops Dino

    1 Aggieceratops Dino / Person

    Each member can be awarded 1 Aggieceratops Dino, ever.

  • Special Dino(s)

    1 of Each Type / Person

    This applies to each type of special dino ever that has dropped.

What To Do After Finding a Dino

Found a Triceratops?

After finding a triceratops, make a post in the #found-triceratops text channel. This post should include a photo proof of the triceratops.

Found an Aggieceratops?

After finding an Aggieceratops dino, make a post in the #found-aggie text channel. This post should include a photo proof of the triceratops.
