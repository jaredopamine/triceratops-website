Our Mission

We bring fun to the Davis community!

Hey, we’re the triceratops club at UC Davis! Our purpose is to create a fun and welcoming community of members that all search for one thing: cute, handmade, crocheted triceratops.

We host daily scavenger hunts and monthly large-scale events for members to search for and claim exclusive dinos, which offers UCD students opportunities to meet new people or to spend quality time with their friends.

We aim to bring students together, explore, and better the campus through engaging in a stress free, enjoyable activity that serves as an escape from the average busy college student’s life.

Our Values

We ensure that every dino-thusiast is welcome to the Dino Community!

Inclusivity

Don’t know anyone in the club? Don’t know how to crochet? Every dino-thusiast is welcome to join!

We encourage students from all backgrounds to join and be involved in the dino community!

Accessibility

Worried that you have to pay member-fees? Being a dino-thusiast is free! We want to make sure being a part of the community is accessible!

*For any flash-sale raffles and purchases, there are still payments required.

Flexibility

Worried about heavy time commitments? Being a dino-thusiast is flexible!

Many of our events and drops are flexible and we don’t require attendance at meetings! You can be involved at your own time and convenience!
